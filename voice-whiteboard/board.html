IDEA BOARD connecting

Think out loud.

Ask for shapes, definitions, code, connections or motion. The board responds while the conversation continues.

Drag an object to move it. Ctrl-click selects one piece inside a composite. Drag the corner handle of a selected object to resize it. Drag empty space to pan. Wheel to zoom. Double-click a shape to edit its text. Shift-click to select several objects.

Point (M, or Alt-click): each click leaves a numbered mark the model can read — "link 1 and 2", "move these to 3". Type a note after clicking, Enter to keep it. Marks fade once the model has read them.

3D unlocks the camera: right-drag to orbit, drag empty space to pan, wheel to zoom. Faces (polygons with [x,y,z] points), cubes and pyramids have depth; cards and text always face you. Motion pauses while in 3D.

Space pauses motion. Ctrl-Z undoes. Delete removes selected objects.
